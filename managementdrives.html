The order in which I work.

Management Drives distinguishes six drives. What counts is not which ones you have, but the order in which they switch on. For me that logic looks like this.

1

Red: nerve, pace and force

Action comes first: making the call, keeping up the pace and being there at the moment it gets uncomfortable.

2

Orange: focused on the goal and the route

Once there is movement, the goal comes into focus: what do we want to achieve, and what is the fastest route there.

3

Yellow: analysing and adjusting

Tuning the engine while driving: understanding why something works, and adjusting it as soon as it can be better.

4

Blue: structure and completion

Structure is a means for me: I bring it in when it helps to actually finish something.

What I bring, and where I need pushback

I am focused on action, combined with ambition: I genuinely want to achieve something and take responsibility when it has to happen. What I value in a team is pushback: someone who reminds me that alignment is not delay, and that certainty and trust weigh more heavily for others than they do for me.
