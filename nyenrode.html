The choice: Financial Management.

Nyenrode offers three tracks: Global Business, Digital Business & Innovation and Financial Management. I chose the last one, because it is the only one that answers the question every improvement proposal ultimately lives or dies by: what does it return, and how do we pay for it.

Block 34 ECTS

Mergers, Acquisitions and Private Equity

How an acquisition comes about: valuing, financing and structuring, and what still has to be merged once the ink is dry.

Block 34 ECTS

International Money and Finance

Currencies, interest rates and capital flows between countries, and how a company hedges the risks that come with them.

Block 44 ECTS

Asset Management & Financial Markets

How capital is invested and priced in financial markets, and how return relates to the risk taken for it.

Block 5 · incl. group project5 ECTS

Advanced Corporate Finance

Financing decisions at board level: capital structure, investment headroom and value creation, concluded with the group project.

Three things I take from this master's.

Financial

Being able to price value

Translating a plan into a valuation, an investment proposal or a capital requirement. Not only whether something improves, but by how much and at what cost.

Strategic

Choices under uncertainty

Deciding when the figures are incomplete: weighing scenarios, naming risk and still choosing a direction that can be defended.

Organisation

Making strategy land

Why good plans run aground in execution, and what behaviour, structure and leadership do to that.
